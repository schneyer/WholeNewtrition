Quick Reminders

  • Protein first.
  • Carbs are fine; pair them with protein.
  • Keep portions small and repeat later.
  • Go easy on pistachios, hummus, coconut, and oily fish if digestion is touchy.
  • Add chicken, turkey, shrimp, or ground turkey to soup if it seems light.

This is a practical meal aid, not a substitute for the surgical or oncology team's instructions.
