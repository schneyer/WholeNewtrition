# Questions For Surgical / Oncology Team

## Blood Sugar

- What glucose range do you want before meals?
- What glucose range do you want at bedtime?
- At what number should we give insulin?
- At what number should we call you?
- What symptoms of low blood sugar should we watch for?
- Is insulin expected to be temporary, or is it too early to know?
- Should he see endocrinology or diabetes education?
- Should we change food choices based on glucose readings, or mainly follow the insulin plan for now?

## Food / Nutrition

- How many calories should we aim for right now?
- How many grams of protein should we aim for right now?
- Are there any foods specifically restricted after his surgery?
- Is moderate carbohydrate intake okay if glucose is monitored?
- Should we use diabetes-specific shakes like Glucerna, or regular high-protein low-sugar shakes?
- Should he meet with an oncology dietitian?
- Are small frequent meals preferred for the next few weeks?

## Pancreatic Enzymes / Digestion

- Does he need pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy, such as Creon or Zenpep?
- What symptoms would suggest he is not digesting fat well?
- If he has greasy, floating, pale, or foul-smelling stools, should we call?
- If he has diarrhea, gas, bloating, or weight loss, should we call?
- If enzymes are prescribed, should he take them with every meal and snack?
- Are there fat limits right now, or should we adjust based on symptoms?

## Weight / Recovery

- How often should we weigh him?
- How much weight loss would concern you?
- What appetite changes are normal 8 to 10 days after surgery?
- How much walking or activity should he be doing now?
- Are there signs that he is under-eating even if he says he feels okay?

## Splenectomy

- Which vaccines has he received already?
- Which vaccines does he still need, and when?
- What is the fever protocol now that he does not have a spleen?
- At what temperature should we call you?
- At what temperature should we go to the ER?
- Should he have emergency antibiotics at home?
- Are there sick-contact precautions we should follow?
- Are there travel or animal-bite precautions we should know about?
