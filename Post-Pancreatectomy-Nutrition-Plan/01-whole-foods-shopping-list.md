# Whole Foods Shopping List

Goal: buy many easy, normal, appetizing options so Dad can eat enough while recovering.

Avoid relying on eggs, cheese, yogurt, cottage cheese, or mushy foods.

## Prepared / Ready-To-Eat Proteins

- Rotisserie chicken
- Grilled chicken breasts or chicken skewers
- Prepared salmon filets
- Mild turkey meatballs
- Turkey breast slices from the deli
- Roast beef slices
- Shrimp cocktail
- Chicken salad, if not too mayo-heavy
- Tuna salad, if not too mayo-heavy
- Mild turkey chili
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Turkey or chicken vegetable soup
- Bone broth or high-protein sipping broth

## Easy Cook-at-Home Proteins

- Salmon filets
- Cod, halibut, or other mild white fish
- Chicken breasts or thighs
- Ground turkey
- Turkey burgers
- Lean beef patties
- Pork tenderloin
- Mild chicken sausage
- Frozen grilled chicken strips
- Canned tuna
- Tuna packets
- Salmon packets
- Sardines, only if he likes them

## Low-Sugar Protein Drinks

Look for high protein and low added sugar.

- Fairlife Core Power, 26g or 42g protein
- Premier Protein shakes
- OWYN protein shakes
- Orgain Clean Protein, lower-sugar versions
- Ensure Max Protein
- Glucerna shakes, especially if the team prefers diabetes-oriented shakes
- Unflavored whey protein
- Collagen peptides, useful but not a complete protein by itself

## Protein Snacks

- Turkey roll-ups, no cheese
- Tuna or salmon packets with crackers
- Low-sugar jerky, ideally softer texture
- Roasted edamame
- Hummus with pita or crackers, if texture is tolerated
- Peanut butter packets
- Almond butter packets
- Mixed nuts, lightly salted
- Low-sugar protein bars: RXBAR, Aloha, Think!, Quest, Barebells
- Nut butter on toast or crackers

## Moderate Carbs

Carbs are not the enemy. Pair them with protein.

- Plain or low-sugar oatmeal cups
- Rolled oats
- Sweet potatoes
- Yukon gold or red potatoes
- Microwave rice cups
- Quinoa cups
- Whole grain bread
- Sourdough bread
- Whole wheat English muffins
- Whole grain crackers
- Pita bread
- Low-sugar granola
- Small bananas
- Berries
- Apples
- Pears
- Clementines
- Frozen berries
- Unsweetened applesauce cups, only if texture is okay

## Vegetables / Sides

Cooked vegetables may be easier than raw vegetables early on.

- Green beans
- Carrots
- Zucchini
- Broccoli florets, cooked well
- Baby spinach
- Roasted vegetable trays
- Steam-in-bag vegetables
- Avocados, if fat is tolerated
- Cucumbers
- Tomato soup, check sugar
- Low-sodium vegetable soup
- Simple salad kits, use dressing lightly

## Healthy Calorie Boosters

Use these to prevent weight loss, but go slowly if fat bothers digestion.

- Olive oil
- Avocado oil
- Peanut butter
- Almond butter
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Tahini
- Avocado
- Low-sugar trail mix

## Breakfast Options, No Eggs / No Cheese

- Oatmeal plus protein powder plus berries
- Turkey sausage plus toast plus fruit
- Peanut butter toast plus protein shake
- Smoked salmon on toast or crackers
- Chicken sausage plus sweet potato
- Whole grain waffle plus peanut butter plus berries
- Oatmeal cup plus turkey slices

## Lunch / Dinner Convenience Meals

- Rotisserie chicken plus rice cup plus green beans
- Salmon plus sweet potato plus spinach
- Turkey chili plus crackers
- Chicken soup plus toast
- Turkey sandwich plus fruit
- Tuna packet plus crackers plus cucumber
- Turkey meatballs plus rice plus vegetables
- Mild chicken sausage plus potato plus cooked vegetables
- Frozen meals with 20g+ protein and low added sugar

## Hydration

- Water
- Sparkling water, unsweetened
- Low/no-sugar electrolyte packets: LMNT, Nuun Sport, Liquid I.V. sugar-free
- Herbal tea
- Broth

## Buy Less Of For Now

- Juice
- Soda
- Sweet tea
- Candy
- Cookies, cakes, pastries, ice cream
- Big greasy fried foods
- Very spicy meals
- Huge pasta or rice portions without protein
- Smoothies made mostly from fruit juice
- Giant raw salads if digestion is touchy
