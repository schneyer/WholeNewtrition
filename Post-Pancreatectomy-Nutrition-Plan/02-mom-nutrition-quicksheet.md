# Dad's Food Plan Right Now

The goal is simple:

**Help him heal, keep his weight steady, keep protein high, avoid big sugar hits, and watch digestion.**

## What Matters Most

- Offer small meals or snacks every 2 to 3 hours.
- Include protein every time he eats.
- Do not worry about perfect meals.
- A few bites often is still a win.
- Carbs are allowed. Pair them with protein.
- Avoid sugar drinks and desserts for now.
- Watch his weight, stool, appetite, and glucose numbers.

## Green Light Foods

- Chicken, turkey, fish, salmon, shrimp
- Rotisserie chicken
- Turkey slices
- Meatballs
- Low-sugar protein shakes
- Oatmeal
- Rice
- Potatoes and sweet potatoes
- Berries, bananas, apples, clementines
- Chicken or turkey soups
- Toast, crackers, English muffins
- Cooked vegetables
- Peanut butter, nuts, olive oil, avocado if tolerated

## Yellow Light Foods

These can be useful, but use reasonable portions or watch tolerance.

- Rice, bread, potatoes, oatmeal
- Nuts, avocado, olive oil
- Raw vegetables and salads
- Beans and lentils
- Protein bars
- Fruit smoothies

## Red Light Foods For Now

- Juice
- Soda
- Sweet tea
- Candy
- Cookies, cake, pastries, ice cream as routine foods
- Big greasy fried meals
- Huge meals
- Sugar-free candy or keto sweets that cause gas or diarrhea
- Expired foods, stale opened snacks, or questionable leftovers
- Alcohol unless the care team says okay
- Dieting so strictly that he does not eat enough

## Quick Pantry Sweep

Do this once, calmly.

- **Throw out:** sugary drinks, candy, dessert snacks, expired foods, questionable leftovers.
- **Move out of sight:** chips, snack crackers, sweets, and foods that invite grazing.
- **Keep:** soups, crackers, rice, potatoes, fruit, nuts, protein shakes, and easy proteins.
- **Do not worry about perfection:** a useful protein shake or soup is okay even if the label is not perfect.

## Easy Sample Day

Breakfast:
- Oatmeal with protein powder and berries

Snack:
- Low-sugar protein shake

Lunch:
- Turkey sandwich and fruit

Snack:
- Crackers with peanut butter or tuna packet

Dinner:
- Salmon or rotisserie chicken, sweet potato, cooked vegetables

Evening snack:
- Protein shake, turkey slices, or toast with peanut butter

## When To Call The Care Team

- Fever or chills
- Vomiting or cannot keep fluids down
- New or worsening belly pain
- Diarrhea that continues or is severe
- Greasy, floating, pale, or very foul-smelling stools
- Fast weight loss
- He cannot eat enough
- Blood sugar repeatedly above or below the range the team gave you
- Confusion, weakness, dizziness, or signs of dehydration
- Redness, drainage, or worsening pain around the incision

## About Insulin Right Now

Do not panic about insulin. After pancreatic surgery, blood sugar can be temporarily unstable. Ask the care team what numbers they want and follow their plan.
