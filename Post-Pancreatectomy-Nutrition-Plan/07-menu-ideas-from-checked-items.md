# Quick Menu From What We Have

Use this when nobody wants to think. Pick **one protein**, add **one carb**, and keep the portion small.

## What We Have

Proteins:
- Rotisserie chicken
- Turkey breast slices
- Shrimp cocktail
- Mild turkey chili
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Turkey/chicken vegetable soup
- Salmon filets
- Ground turkey
- Lean beef patties

Carbs / sides:
- Sweet potatoes
- Small bananas
- Pita or crackers
- Tomato soup

Snacks / extras:
- Hummus
- Mixed nuts

## Easy Choices

- **Chicken plate:** rotisserie chicken + sweet potato + tomato soup
- **Soup upgrade:** chicken soup + extra rotisserie chicken
- **Turkey snack plate:** turkey slices + pita/crackers + hummus
- **Shrimp plate:** shrimp cocktail + crackers/pita + a few bites of banana
- **Turkey chili bowl:** mild turkey chili + sweet potato
- **Salmon dinner:** salmon filet + sweet potato + soup
- **Burger plate:** lean beef patty + sweet potato + tomato soup
- **Ground turkey skillet:** ground turkey + diced sweet potato + mild seasoning

## If Appetite Is Low

Start with one of these:

- Chicken soup with extra chicken
- Turkey slices with crackers
- Shrimp cocktail
- A few bites of rotisserie chicken and sweet potato
- Small turkey chili bowl

## Buy More When Low

Most useful restocks:
- Rotisserie chicken or prepared chicken
- Turkey slices
- Protein shakes
- Sweet potatoes
- Crackers or pita
- Easy soup
- Berries or small bananas
- Mild cooked vegetables

## Quick Reminders

- Protein first.
- Carbs are fine; pair them with protein.
- Keep portions small and repeat later.
- Go easy on nuts and hummus if digestion is touchy.
- Add chicken, turkey, shrimp, or ground turkey to soup if it seems light.
