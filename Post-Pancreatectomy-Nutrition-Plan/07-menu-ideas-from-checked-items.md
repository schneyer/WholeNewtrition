# Menu Ideas From Current Checked Items

Based on the checked shopping-list items:

## Current Useful Inventory

Prepared proteins:
- Rotisserie chicken
- Turkey breast slices from the deli
- Shrimp cocktail
- Mild turkey chili
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Turkey or chicken vegetable soup

Cook-at-home proteins:
- Salmon filets
- Ground turkey
- Lean beef patties

Snacks:
- Hummus with pita or crackers, if texture is tolerated
- Mixed nuts, lightly salted

Carbs:
- Sweet potatoes
- Small bananas

Vegetable / side:
- Tomato soup, check sugar

## Easy Meals

Rotisserie chicken plate:
- Rotisserie chicken
- Sweet potato
- Optional tomato soup on the side

Turkey sandwich plate:
- Turkey breast slices
- Pita or crackers
- Hummus
- Small banana if he wants something sweet

Soup plus extra protein:
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Add rotisserie chicken if the soup is light on protein

Shrimp snack plate:
- Shrimp cocktail
- Crackers or pita
- Small banana or a few nuts

Turkey chili bowl:
- Mild turkey chili
- Sweet potato on the side
- Add extra ground turkey later if making a bigger batch

Salmon dinner:
- Salmon filet
- Sweet potato
- Vegetable soup or tomato soup

Burger plate:
- Lean beef patty
- Sweet potato
- Tomato soup or vegetable soup

Ground turkey skillet:
- Ground turkey
- Diced sweet potato
- Mild seasoning
- Serve with a little hummus or soup if he wants moisture

## Small Frequent Meal Ideas

- Turkey slices plus crackers
- Rotisserie chicken plus a few bites of sweet potato
- Shrimp plus small banana
- Chicken soup with extra chicken
- Hummus with pita plus turkey slices
- Small turkey chili bowl
- Mixed nuts plus protein shake, if available
- Tomato soup plus turkey slices

## 2-Day Simple Plan

Day 1:
- Breakfast: small banana plus turkey slices
- Snack: shrimp cocktail with crackers
- Lunch: chicken soup with extra rotisserie chicken
- Snack: hummus with pita or crackers
- Dinner: salmon filet with sweet potato
- Evening: tomato soup or a few bites of turkey chili

Day 2:
- Breakfast: turkey slices plus small sweet potato
- Snack: mixed nuts and a few bites of banana
- Lunch: turkey chili bowl
- Snack: rotisserie chicken bites
- Dinner: lean beef patty with sweet potato and vegetable soup
- Evening: chicken/turkey vegetable soup

## Best Defaults When Appetite Is Low

- Soup plus extra protein
- Rotisserie chicken plus sweet potato
- Turkey slices plus crackers
- Shrimp cocktail
- Mild turkey chili

## Watchouts

- Keep portions small at first.
- Pair banana, sweet potato, pita, or crackers with protein.
- Go easy on nuts and hummus if fat or fiber bothers digestion.
- If soups are low-protein, add chicken, turkey, shrimp, or ground turkey.
