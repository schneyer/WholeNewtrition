# Quick Menu From What We Have

Use this when nobody wants to think. Pick **one protein**, add **one carb or soup**, and keep the portion small.

## What We Have

Proteins:
- Rotisserie chicken
- Turkey breast slices
- Shrimp ring with cocktail sauce
- Salmon filets
- Ground turkey
- Lean beef patties
- Sardines, several types
- Smoked kippers
- Pickled herring

Soups / bowls:
- Mild beef chili
- Beef and barley soup, he likes this
- Tomato soup
- Chicken noodle soup, not tried yet
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Turkey/chicken vegetable soup

Carbs / sides:
- Sweet potatoes
- Small bananas
- Pita or crackers
- Husked corn, boil or grill
- Grilled vegetable medley
- Peppers
- Carrot sticks

Snacks / fruit / treats:
- Hummus
- Blackberries
- Strawberries
- Pistachios
- Grilled pineapple, dessert treat
- Coconut, he likes coconut generally but not the batch we bought
- Jicama, he likes it, but the batch we bought went bad

## Easy Choices

- **Chicken plate:** rotisserie chicken + sweet potato + tomato soup
- **Soup upgrade:** chicken noodle/chicken soup + extra rotisserie chicken
- **Beef barley lunch:** beef and barley soup + turkey slices or chicken on the side
- **Chili bowl:** mild beef chili + sweet potato or corn
- **Turkey snack plate:** turkey slices + pita/crackers + hummus
- **Shrimp plate:** shrimp ring + cocktail sauce + crackers/pita + berries
- **Salmon dinner:** salmon filet + sweet potato + grilled vegetables
- **Burger plate:** lean beef patty + sweet potato + tomato soup
- **Ground turkey skillet:** ground turkey + peppers + diced sweet potato
- **Tinned fish plate:** sardines/kippers/herring + crackers + carrots or peppers
- **Snack bowl:** berries + pistachios
- **Treat:** grilled pineapple after a protein-forward meal

## If Appetite Is Low

Start with one of these:

- Beef and barley soup
- Chicken soup with extra chicken
- Turkey slices with crackers
- Shrimp cocktail
- A few bites of rotisserie chicken and sweet potato
- Small chili bowl
- Berries and pistachios

## Quick Reminders

- Protein first.
- Carbs are fine; pair them with protein.
- Keep portions small and repeat later.
- Go easy on pistachios, hummus, coconut, and oily fish if digestion is touchy.
- Add chicken, turkey, shrimp, or ground turkey to soup if it seems light.
