# Post-Pancreatic Surgery Nutrition Plan

For Dad, age 73, recovering from distal pancreatectomy and splenectomy for pancreatic cancer.

Core theme:

**Keep him eating, keep protein high, avoid sugar bombs, monitor glucose, and watch digestion/weight.**

## Current Assumptions

- Recovery and adequate intake matter more than strict carb restriction.
- Protein should appear at every meal or snack.
- Carbs are allowed in reasonable portions, especially paired with protein.
- Avoid obvious sugar bombs: juice, soda, candy, desserts, sweetened drinks.
- Small frequent meals are likely easier than large meals.
- Watch for signs of pancreatic enzyme insufficiency: greasy/floating stools, urgent diarrhea, bloating, or weight loss.
- Splenectomy mainly changes infection/vaccine/fever vigilance, not the grocery list.
