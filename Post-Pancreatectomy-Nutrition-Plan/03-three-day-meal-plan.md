# 3-Day Mix-and-Match Meal Plan

Use this loosely. Swap foods around. The goal is 5 to 6 small eating moments per day, not perfection.

No eggs. No cheese-heavy meals. Protein-forward. Moderate carbs.

## Day 1

Breakfast:
- Oatmeal with protein powder stirred in
- Berries

Snack:
- Low-sugar protein shake

Lunch:
- Rotisserie chicken
- Rice cup
- Cooked green beans

Snack:
- Whole grain crackers
- Tuna packet

Dinner:
- Salmon
- Sweet potato
- Spinach or zucchini

Evening snack:
- Peanut butter toast or turkey slices with fruit

## Day 2

Breakfast:
- Turkey sausage
- Whole wheat English muffin
- Small banana

Snack:
- Low-sugar protein shake

Lunch:
- Turkey sandwich on sourdough or whole grain bread
- Apple slices

Snack:
- Hummus with pita or crackers
- Alternative: nut butter packet with crackers

Dinner:
- Turkey meatballs
- Rice or quinoa
- Cooked carrots

Evening snack:
- Low-sugar protein bar
- Alternative: broth and toast

## Day 3

Breakfast:
- Peanut butter toast
- Berries
- Protein shake if appetite is low

Snack:
- Shrimp cocktail or turkey roll-ups

Lunch:
- Chicken soup with rice or noodles
- Crackers

Snack:
- Small portion of nuts or trail mix
- Alternative: tuna or salmon packet

Dinner:
- Mild turkey chili or chicken sausage
- Potato
- Cooked vegetables

Evening snack:
- Oatmeal cup, protein shake, or toast with almond butter

## Mix-and-Match Formula

Pick one from each group.

Protein:
- Chicken
- Turkey
- Fish
- Shrimp
- Tuna
- Salmon
- Protein shake

Carb:
- Oatmeal
- Rice
- Potato
- Sweet potato
- Toast
- Crackers
- Fruit

Vegetable or Fruit:
- Cooked vegetables
- Berries
- Apple
- Clementine

Calories:
- Olive oil
- Peanut butter
- Nuts
- Avocado, if tolerated

## Backup Meals For Low-Appetite Moments

- Protein shake plus toast
- Chicken soup plus crackers
- Turkey slices plus fruit
- Tuna packet plus crackers
- Peanut butter toast
- Rotisserie chicken plus rice cup
